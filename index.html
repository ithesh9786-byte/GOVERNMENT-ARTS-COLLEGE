About Government Arts College, C. Mutlur

Government Arts College, C. Mutlur (Chidambaram) is a public co-educational institution established in 1982 to serve rural students and first-generation learners. The college offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programmes across various disciplines and is committed to providing quality higher education.

Location & History

  • Location: C. Mutlur, 8 km from Chidambaram, Cuddalore District, Tamil Nadu – 608102.
  • Established: 1982.
  • Campus: Shifted to the present C. Mutlur campus in August 1987.
  • Affiliation: Affiliated with Thiruvalluvar University, Vellore.
  • Accreditation: Accredited by NAAC.

Academic Programmes

The college offers more than 30 programmes including B.A., B.Sc., B.Com., BBA, BCA, M.A., M.Sc., M.Com., M.Phil. and Ph.D. in various disciplines.

Infrastructure

  • Modern classrooms
  • Computer laboratories
  • General library
  • Academic and administrative blocks
  • Sports facilities
  • NSS & NCC activities

Vision

To empower rural students through quality education, discipline, research, and social responsibility.

Mission

To provide affordable higher education, encourage innovation, develop employability skills, and promote ethical values among students.
